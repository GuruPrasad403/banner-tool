Indication and Usage

NEXICOR® (vibrostatine) is indicated for the treatment of moderate-to-severe symptomatic disease in adults who have had an inadequate response to first-line therapeutic regimens.

Important Safety Information

WARNING: RISK OF SERIOUS INFECTIONS AND HEPATIC DYSFUNCTION

Patients treated with NEXICOR are at increased risk for developing serious bacterial, viral, and fungal infections that may lead to hospitalization or fatal outcomes.

Contraindications

NEXICOR is contraindicated in patients with known severe hypersensitivity to vibrostatine or any of the excipients in the formulation, as well as in patients with severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh Class C).

Warnings and Precautions

Hepatic Monitoring: Elevated serum transaminases (ALT and AST) greater than 3 times the upper limit of normal have been observed in clinical trials. Evaluate liver enzymes prior to initiation and periodically during treatment.

Cardiovascular Events: In controlled studies, a higher rate of major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) was reported in patients aged 50 years and older with at least one cardiovascular risk factor.

Thrombosis: Deep vein thrombosis (DVT) and pulmonary embolism (PE) have been reported in clinical studies. Patients with symptoms of thrombosis should be promptly evaluated.

Adverse Reactions

The most common adverse reactions occurring in ≥5% of treated patients include upper respiratory tract infections, headache, nausea, elevated liver enzymes, and fatigue.

  • Nasopharyngitis (12.4%)
  • Headache (8.1%)
  • Nausea and dyspepsia (6.3%)
  • Alanine aminotransferase increased (5.5%)

Drug Interactions

Co-administration with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors increases exposure to vibrostatine. Dosage reduction is recommended when co-administered. Avoid concomitant use with strong CYP3A4 inducers.

Dosage and Administration

The recommended dosage of NEXICOR is 20 mg taken orally once daily with or without food. Refer to full Prescribing Information for renal and hepatic dose adjustments.
