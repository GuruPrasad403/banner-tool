IMPORTANT SAFETY INFORMATION

WARNING: RISK OF SERIOUS CARDIOVASCULAR AND GASTROINTESTINAL EVENTS. See full prescribing information for complete boxed warning.
INDICATION: CLINICAL-X is indicated for the treatment of moderate to severe chronic inflammation in adult patients who have had an inadequate response to conventional therapy.
CONTRAINDICATIONS: CLINICAL-X is contraindicated in patients with known hypersensitivity to any component of the formulation, or patients with active severe systemic infections.
WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS: Infections: Serious and sometimes fatal infections due to bacterial, mycobacterial, invasive fungal, viral, or other opportunistic pathogens have been reported in patients receiving immunosuppressive agents.
ADVERSE REACTIONS: The most common adverse reactions (incidence ≥ 5%) observed in clinical trials included nasopharyngitis, upper respiratory tract infection, headache, nausea, and mild elevation of hepatic enzymes.
USE IN SPECIFIC POPULATIONS: Pregnancy: There are no adequate and well-controlled studies in pregnant women. CLINICAL-X should be used during pregnancy only if the potential benefit justifies the potential risk to the fetus. Nursing Mothers: Caution should be exercised when administered to a nursing woman.
DRUG INTERACTIONS: Co-administration with strong CYP3A4 inhibitors may increase systemic exposure. Monitor patients closely for adverse reactions when initiating concomitant therapy.
PATIENT COUNSELING INFORMATION: Advise the patient to read the FDA-approved patient labeling (Medication Guide). Instruct patients to report signs of infection or unusual bleeding immediately.
